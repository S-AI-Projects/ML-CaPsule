## Description
Implements the simplest honest regression baseline (training-mean predictor) for the Auto MPG regression teaching component. The baseline fits its sole parameter (mean of training targets = 4.0) exclusively on training records r1..r4, then evaluates MAE and MSE on held-out validation (r5) and test (r6) partitions without accessing test data for fitting. An oracle linear comparator demonstrates zero-MSE contrast.
