## Description

Implements and verifies an explicit, leak-proof numeric preprocessing contract for the **Zoo animals – decision tree** teaching component (`course-labs/r41-t025`). Numeric standardization statistics (mean, population standard deviation, and zero-variance-safe scale) are fitted exclusively on training rows (`r1, r2, r5, r6`) and returned as a frozen, transform-only `FittedNumericPreprocessor` with no `fit`, `partial_fit`, or `fit_transform` method — making fitting on held-out input structurally impossible through the public transform interface.

Feature order (`["x1", "x2"]`), integer label set (`{0, 1}`), missing-value policy (`reject` by default; optional training-mean-only `impute_train_mean`), and train/validation/test contamination guards are all explicitly enforced and unit-tested on an offline, CPU-only, eight-row synthetic fixture.
