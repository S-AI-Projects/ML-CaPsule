## Description

Implements and verifies a structured **error-analysis report** and bounded Multinomial Naive Bayes text baseline for the **Twitter airline sentiment – Naive Bayes** teaching component (`course-labs/r41-t028`). Each misclassified record is reported with its stable `record_id`, `expected_value`, `predicted_value`, `split`, and controlled `reason_category`, while correctly classified records are strictly excluded from error rows.

To make the error-analysis pipeline end-to-end testable on the six-record synthetic fixture (`t1..t6`), one deliberately wrong prediction is injected on `t5` (expected `1`, flipped to `0`, tagged `injected_test_error`), verifying that `t5` appears in the report, correct predictions (such as `t6` and `t1..t4`) do not, and every aggregate counter reconciles exactly with the detailed rows.
